What makes this mock difficult

Reasoning-weighted

90% of items sit above simple recall. The exact Bloom mix is 6 Remember, 12 Understand, 18 Apply, 15 Analyze, 6 Evaluate, and 3 Create.

Cross-module traps

Items combine prompting, validation, product selection, governance, configuration, and troubleshooting so a technically true answer can still be wrong for the situation.

Case-study continuity

Questions 41-50 share Asteron Components, the same project, stakeholders, data boundaries, workflow, and decision ownership.

Post-submit audit trail

Review includes the correct answer, why every option is right or wrong, lesson, concept, Bloom level, difficulty, confidence, and official-source path.

Rules

Before you begin

  1. Single-choice items require one answer. Multiple-response items state exactly how many answers to select.
  2. Multiple-response scoring is all-or-nothing. Unanswered items score as incorrect.
  3. You may flag, clear, and revisit questions until submission or time expiry.
  4. Answers and rationales are revealed only after submission.
  5. Your attempt is stored in this browser so an accidental refresh can be resumed.
